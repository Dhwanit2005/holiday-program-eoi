Make the school holidays count.

A small-group tutoring program for Year 11 and Year 12 students, run by two tutors. Parents and students can register in about three minutes. Registering doesn't commit you to anything. We'll email the info pack straight away.

How it works

  1. Register your interest. Three short steps: the student, the courses, then a parent or guardian.
  2. Get the info pack. It's emailed to the parent or guardian straight away, and shown on screen.
  3. We confirm the details. Once the dates are final, we contact you. You then decide whether to confirm a place.

Common questions

Does registering commit us?

No. This only tells us you're interested.

Who should fill this in?

Either the student or a parent or guardian. We email the info pack to the parent or guardian's address.

What if places are full?

You'll be added to the waitlist and we'll contact you if a place opens up.

Who do I contact with questions?
